Privacy Policy

Birdie · Last updated 2 August 2026

Birdie is a golf round tracker for iPhone. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: Birdie does not collect your data, and nothing you enter leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Birdie has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no third-party SDKs. We do not operate a server that receives your information, and we cannot see anything you enter.

What Birdie stores on your device

The rounds, courses, tee boxes, scores, and display preferences you create are saved in a private database inside the app on your iPhone. This information stays on your device and is only accessible to you.

If your iPhone is backed up to iCloud or a computer, that backup includes Birdie's data, in the same way it includes data from other apps. Those backups are controlled by you and by Apple, not by us.

Purchases

Birdie offers an optional subscription. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether a subscription is currently active. Apple's handling of purchase information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Data sharing

Because Birdie does not collect information, there is nothing to share, sell, or disclose to anyone.

Children

Birdie is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your iPhone removes its database and everything in it. Because we hold no copy of your information, there is nothing for you to request from us and nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Birdie adds anything that changes this — for example optional iCloud sync between your own devices — this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
