Common questions

How do I record a round?
Tap Start Round, pick a course and the tees you're playing, then enter each hole as you go. Your progress saves automatically after every hole, so you can lock your phone or close the app mid-round and pick up exactly where you left off.
Can I fix a score I entered wrong?
Yes. During a round, open the scorecard and tap any hole to edit it. On the round summary screen — before you submit — tap any hole to correct it. After a round is saved, open it from History and choose Edit Scorecard.
Why doesn't my round count toward my handicap?
A round only counts if the tees you played have both a slope rating and a course rating saved, and the round was played to completion. Rounds you end early never count. You can add ratings later by editing the course, and Tidy Golf will ask whether past rounds from those tees should be included.
How is my handicap calculated?
Tidy Golf uses the World Handicap System method: each qualifying round produces a score differential of (113 ÷ slope) × (score − course rating), and your index is the average of your best differentials from your most recent 20 rounds, following the official adjustment table. Net double bogey adjustment and the Playing Conditions Calculation are not applied.
Where is my data stored?
Entirely on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded. See the privacy policy for details.
What does the subscription include?
Your first round is free. A subscription lets you keep tracking unlimited rounds and gets you new features first. Your history and statistics remain readable whether or not you subscribe.
How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. You can cancel there at any time. If you're in a free trial, cancelling at least 24 hours before it ends means you won't be charged. Apple handles all billing and refunds — see Apple's refund page.
I paid but the app doesn't recognise it.
Open the Premium screen and tap Restore Purchases. If that doesn't work, email us.
